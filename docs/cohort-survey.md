# Cohort survey, 10 October 2026

Every final-project app in the course is at
`https://comp4020-final-<handle>.fly.dev`, and `/readme/` serves its README.
I fetched all 124 handles on 10 October (an earlier pass on 4 October found
only 31 real READMEs). Agents read the 114 real READMEs in five batches,
each answering three questions: can one person do the core thing alone, what
changes with 2, 3 or many people, and what is left behind. Nothing was
posted to anyone's app. An earlier pass on 4 October, with the same
read-only rule, is summarised at the end.

## The shape of the class

- 124 handles, 117 deployed, 114 with a real README, 109 repos public.
- The six course crit agents (bada, baishi, dachi, liuru, shitao, yunlin)
  all built an append-only wall or scroll. That is this cohort's median
  answer.
- 14 of the first 32 READMEs cited Robin Sloan's "An app can be a
  home-cooked meal"; 6 cited Shirky's "Situated Software".
- Nobody requires three or more people for one act, and nobody makes the
  number of people needed change with how many are there.

## Designs where a second person is a rule, not a hope

- **Calligraphy Relay** (u7663394): one brush, one character each, and
  nobody may write two in a row. A scroll can't be finished alone. The
  finished scroll keeps a red seal per writer. Real-time now.
- **The village** (anpham-09): a thing placed in a room reaches the house's
  permanent shelf only if someone else was in the room at the time, and the
  shelf entry carries both names and the day.
- **Skyline** (aaronjin0323): one tile per person on a 12 × 12 board; a
  tile's height is the number of claimed neighbours. Alone you stay flat.
- **The garden** (cxin16215-netizen): nothing grows while one person is
  there; 2, 3, 4 and 5 people unlock colour families. The garden is kept.
  Others' changes still arrive on a 15-second poll.
- **Collective Snake** (brendanmeyer): the game refuses to start with one
  session; each player steers 1/n of one snake.
- **Chain Reaction** (pangxuancong): one shared machine, one mechanism per
  visitor, interdependent settings. The shipped build is one ramp and one
  cup.
- **Dead Air** (mattxreynolds) and **Lost & Found** (2513238602): two seats
  with server-enforced information asymmetry; the other person is the only
  channel. Fixed at two.
- **Binary Ninja, Pairing Bay** (ky787): two bodies on separate plates
  permanently unlock the exit.

## Headcount changing the shape of play

- **Watch YouTube Together** (zdy-forever): alone, a change applies at once;
  with two or more it needs a majority vote.
- **Card Clash** (posture627k): 2 players is 1v1, 3 is 1v2, 4 is 2v2.
- **The Rail** (harry1357): a shared boss fight whose author writes that
  "boss gains HP per joiner" is the answer he is replacing, because more
  people should make the fight different, not longer.

## The artefact as the history

- **Five Windows** (rithikanallaparaju16): the whole house (mess, thirst,
  the dog, an UNO replay) is computed from an append-only log of acts.
- **Theseus** (baizhenh884-dev): one shared sentence; replaced words stay
  faded beneath their position; earlier forms are browsable.
- **Constellation** (chajie0824): declarations between people as an
  append-only event log whose brightness decays; a timeline scrubber.
- **The Dig** (hemansii): a buried web page revealed one cell at a time,
  five digs per person per day, with notes and theories kept in place.
- **drift archive** (marcuszorian): your best crash of the day becomes a
  ghost car where it happened; older days render more worn.
- **Overlap** (mukulsharma0260-alt): one star per browser, brightness
  fading with time since the last visit, never removed.
- **Kessler** (am167): satellites that collide and name who collided; a
  beacon is heard only by who is present as it passes.
- **Common Pool** (avaai666): one net can't empty the pond, two can, and the
  spec proves it; a dead pond stays dead and listed.

## Closest to what Earshot was

- **Guinea Pig Sanctuary** (zephyrieal): the README promises "an interaction
  designed for multiple visitors cannot be completed by one visitor alone";
  the live app is a feed counter and flowers.
- **Shut Up and Read** (supermarine45): the server drops chat sent during a
  focus block, tested by a second socket receiving nothing; the same shape
  of rule as a bubble that doesn't reach 9 tiles.
- **Torchlight** (yimche): server-side fog by torch beam, with a reveal-all
  flash every 10 seconds.

## What this changed

The strongest designs share two things: a rule that makes a second person
necessary, and an artefact that is itself the attributed record. Earshot's
gate had the first for exactly two people and almost none of the second. The
ship design in `docs/the-ship.md` keeps both and adds the thing nobody in
the class has: how many hands a thing needs changes with how far out you
are.

## The 4 October pass

32 apps had real READMEs then (26 students, 6 agents). Agents rated
Theseus, The garden, Calligraphy Relay, Knuckle Road (ruiquanqiao) and
Constellation highest on idea and README, and found nobody limiting
information by position. Six students were real-time across people; most
had deferred it to crit 9. Seventeen of the READMEs cited Robin Sloan.
