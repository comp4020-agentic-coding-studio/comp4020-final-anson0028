# 11. No currency, no reward for a landing

Date: 9 November 2026. Status: accepted, against my own wish.

## Context

I wanted this one. Sea of Thieves pays a crew in gold and reputation for
every voyage, and that loop is a large part of why people come back to it.
A landing here could pay out too: coins for the far island, a flag colour
to buy, a bigger sail. The game already keeps the record a reward would
hang off (decision record 5): every landing and wreck is written with the
crew's names and the day.

## Options

1. **Coins and a shop.** Each landing pays by distance; coins buy cosmetic
   things for your bird or your ship. Familiar, and it gives a reason to
   come back on your own.
2. **Levels.** Experience per voyage, a rank beside your name. Cheaper to
   build than a shop, and it turns the board into a ranking by the back
   door, which decision record 5 rules out.
3. **Nothing but the record.** The flag on the island with your names, the
   line on the board, and the wreck that stays. No number goes up.

## Decision

Option 3. The first bullet of what good means here is that friends get a
good hour together and every job matters. A currency moves the reason to
sail from the people aboard to the number in the corner: the far island
becomes worth more than the crew, the quickest route beats the one with
talk on it, and a player alone grinding coins is doing the opposite of
what the game is for. The record already says what a reward would say,
that you were there and who with, and it says it to everyone.

## Consequences

- There is no progression. The reason to come back is the sea, the far
  island nobody has reached yet, and whoever is at the pier tonight. If
  that is not enough, the game is not good enough, and a shop would hide
  that rather than fix it.
- Nothing in the snapshot or the database counts coins or points, so the
  rule "never add scores" in `CLAUDE.md` stays simple to keep.
- The flag on each island carries the first crew's names for as long as
  the island stands. That is the only trophy, and it cannot be bought or
  lost.
- If I change my mind, it goes through this record first, with the board
  rule (decision record 5) rewritten in the same commit.
