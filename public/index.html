    Earshot

    A small online co-op game. You and whoever else is here right now crew the one ship at the pier, and the more of you aboard, the further it gets.

    You can only do what you stand at. Walk up to the ship and press E to board; you get a job. Then stand at a station and hold E: sail, helm, chart table or pump. Every station stops the moment it is left.

    The harbour board lists how far each crew got, with their names.

    WASD or tap to walk · drag to look · E boards and works a station · H Y B wave and nod
