    Earshot

    A harbour with three ships, and a sea that gets harder further out.

    Board a ship and hold E at a station: sail, helm, chart table, pump. A body holds one station at a time, and every station runs down when it's left. One person can sail, badly. More hands get further.

    Rocks ringed with white water are reefs. One under the hull opens a hole on deck. Stand on it and hold E to patch it, then pump out what came in.

    Where each crew got to goes on the harbour board and the chart, with their names.
