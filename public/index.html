    All Hands on Deck

    A small online co-op game. You are a block bird on a pier, and a galleon is tied up beside you. Whoever is online is its crew: walk up the gangway and press E to board, and when you are enough, sail. The more of you aboard, the faster it gets anywhere.

    You can only work the station you stand at, and what you say is heard by your ship, not by the shore. The helm and chart table are at the stern, the mainsail amidships, the pump beside it, the foresail at the bow. You are told which station is yours. Stand at it and hold E; every station stops the moment it is left. Rocks hole the deck; stand on the hole to patch it.

    The harbour board lists how far each crew got, with their names. E by the gangway steps off again; the pier is where the next crew gathers.

    WASD or tap to walk · arrows or drag to look · E boards and works a station · N leaves a note · H waves · Y and B nod and shake
